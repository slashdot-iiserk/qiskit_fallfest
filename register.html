About the participation fee

There is a participation fee for the fest. The amount has not been announced yet — it will be published here and emailed to everyone who has registered, well before the first session.

Registering on this page costs nothing and commits you to nothing. It is how we reach you: all further communication about the fee, the schedule and the material happens by email and on this website.

What happens next

  • You get the installation guide and pre-reading straight away.
  • Lab notebooks land in your inbox before each session.
  • The Day 4 invited speaker is announced to registrants first.
  • The participation fee is announced by email once it is set.
  • Certificates are emailed after the fest, to this address.

Bring with you

  • A laptop and its charger.
  • Python 3.10 or newer, if you can install it beforehand.
  • Nothing else. Day 1 ends with an installation clinic.

Privacy

This page posts your answers to Google Forms and nowhere else. A draft is kept in your own browser so you do not lose progress; it is cleared once you submit. No analytics or trackers run on this site.
